Paid-review / onboarding bounty policy packet

Keep paid review bounties useful without buying shallow signals.

Small OSS bounties for PR reviews, first bug reports, or onboarding tasks can produce useful maintainer feedback. They also create endorsement, spam, and disclosure risk when claim rules are vague.

This readback gives bounty-board owners a fixed-scope way to tighten the workflow: evidence rules, transparent disclosure, rate limits, and claim language that rewards real review work.

Disclosure

Make paid-review status explicit and tied to actual review evidence, not a standalone endorsement.

Claim Quality

Require exact PRs, files, lines, docs, or issues inspected plus substantive observations.

Spam Control

Use first-review rules, caps, finite pools, duplicate handling, and examples to keep boards readable.

Boundary

This packet does not perform paid reviews, star repositories, claim bounties, post endorsements, accept terms, request payment, or move funds. It prepares a maintainer or board owner to run paid review tasks with clearer public evidence and disclosure rules.
